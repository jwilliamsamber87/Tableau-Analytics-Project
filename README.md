## What’s Inside? 🎉

### 1. Production Throughput & Downtime Dashboard  
- **Goal:** Spot those pesky bottlenecks and boost efficiency  
- **What I’m tracking:** Units/hour, downtime %, and OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness)  
- **Fun stuff:** Interactive charts and downtime Pareto to pinpoint trouble spots  

### 2. Defect Trend Analysis Dashboard  
- **Goal:** See how defect rates change over time so we can keep quality on point  
- **Metrics:** Defect rate per 100 units and monthly changes  
- **Visuals:** Time series plots and colorful stacked bars

### 3. Pallet & Carrier Failure Dashboard  
- **Goal:** Find the pallets or carriers causing the most headaches and reduce scrap  
- **What’s tracked:** Failure rates by pallet and carrier IDs  
- **Features:** Filters and charts to dive deep into patterns

## How to Explore These Dashboards 🖥️

1. Grab the `.twbx` file you want to check out.  
2. Open it with [Tableau Desktop](https://www.tableau.com/products/desktop) or the free [Tableau Reader](https://www.tableau.com/products/reader).  
3. Play around with filters and visuals to see what the data reveals!
